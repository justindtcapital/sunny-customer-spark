# Server utils rules (knowledge harvest, Drive, Gmail)

- Mailbox tokens: per-mailbox Gmail refresh tokens are stored AES-GCM encrypted in the private "Mailbox Tokens" sheet tab, accepted only when Google confirms the consenting account equals GMAIL_KB_MAILBOX — app sign-in is an allowlist, so Google is the identity proof.
- Alias-harvested (gmail-*) activities count as registered activity only after Activity Review confirms them (review state shared with Notes Imports in Harvest State) — keeps automated tagging from polluting dashboards.
- KB Drive meeting files are imported by moving them into Meeting Notes/<PortCo> and marking appProperties.vp=1; files Venture Pulse created carry the same mark and are never re-imported — keeps Drive scans idempotent.
- Network (NWK) notes come from the NWK@ alias (GMAIL_NWK_ALIAS, default nwk@dt-capital.net) or "NWK" in a note title; on review, tagged contacts get one Interaction Trail row each (Source Ref kb:<recordId>, deduped) and the review stores their emails — keeps contact history traceable to the source note.
- New KB files land in Drive 'Unfiled Documents' and move to Meeting Notes/<PortCo>, PortCo Operations or Network Updates only on review — Drive mirrors reviewed state.
- Personal Granola keys are encrypted in Cloud, keyed by verified auth UUID; connection/import operations require a confirmed roster email — typed names cannot select another person's credentials. CRM data stays in Sheets.
- Google Groups rewrite From to the alias; parseMessage unwraps the real sender from X-Original-Sender/Reply-To so per-owner inboxes attribute correctly. Gmail harvest cursors overlap 2 days because existing threads are skipped.
- Contact imports read unified Contacts once for identity checks and append literal cells before exact read-back verification; duplicate view matches and Sheets coercion must not strand successful imports.
- Interrupted imports get a self-service "Check what was saved & unlock" (import-reconciliation planner): it reads fresh Contacts, records per-person evidence and releases only that operation's lock, never writing people or replaying — users recover without risking duplicates.
- Target/Prospecting appends write literal cells so read-back verification matches.
- Morning Brief: read-only enrichment of Claude Cowork's daily email, one Cloud morning_briefs row per owner/day (single-flight claim, email sent once via the linked google_mail connector); cron answers fast and finishes via waitUntil; unknown people go to reviewed paste (per-person or all, also via the emailed /brief?add= link) — never auto-writes CRM. It reads Cowork's full email (searchGmailRaw maxBody; the 3,000-char default cut off the trailing contacts table). People match by Cowork-supplied email before name; nickname-compatible names ("Matthew"/"Matt") are possible matches, never new — prevents duplicate contacts. Each meeting has one subject (title PortCo, else outside company profiled via Google-grounded Gemini); body-only PortCos are name-only mentions and scheduling sentences are stripped — keeps cards about who you're meeting.
- Person event links (Network and Prospecting) share the CRM "Events" tab keyed by Contact URID then email; Prospecting adds are deduped after fresh reads, removals stamp "Removed At" (never delete), and the legacy Targets "Event" cell reads as an Invited link and is never overwritten — so people keep many events and provenance.
- Contact merges keep one record (its URID) and archive the others in place (Relationship Status "Merged", "Merged Into"; email moved to the main record). The main record's "Merged URIDs" pulls archived history in at read time, and the append-only "Contact Merges" tab logs full before/after rows first — no child rows are rewritten, and undo restores only cells nobody edited since.
